Frequently Asked Questions

What happens during a first consultation? expand_more

Your first consultation is an in-depth conversation about your health history, current concerns, and lifestyle. We'll discuss your goals and I may perform a brief physical assessment. From there, we will outline a proposed treatment plan tailored to your individual needs.

Do I need to prepare anything before my visit? expand_more

Please wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing. It's best to have eaten a light meal an hour or two before your appointment. If you have any recent medical reports or imaging relevant to your condition, please bring them along.

How many sessions will I need? expand_more

The number of sessions varies greatly depending on whether your condition is acute or chronic, and how your body responds to treatment. We will discuss a realistic timeline during your initial consultation and review progress regularly. We focus on practitioner-led guidance rather than guarantees.
